Privacy Policy

Voicenotes · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Voicenotes turns what you say into tasks. It is built so that your voice and your tasks stay on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app asks for microphone and speech recognition access so it can hear what you say and turn it into text. Speech is transcribed on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your recordings and transcripts are not sent to us or to any third party. You can choose in Settings to delete recordings after they are processed.

Your tasks

Tasks, dates, and related information are stored only in a database on your device. We cannot see them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content passes through any server.

Export and deletion

In Settings you can export all your data as a file you control, or permanently delete all data from the app.

Purchases

Any in-app purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, by adding advertising), we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
